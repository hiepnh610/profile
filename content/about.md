Hi, I'm **Hiep Nguyen** — a software engineer based in Hanoi, Vietnam.

I spend my working hours on backend systems: designing services, wiring up the infrastructure they run on, and building the tooling that keeps them boring to operate. The common thread in most of what I do is wanting to understand the whole stack under a thing, not just the layer I was hired to touch.

That instinct leaks into my spare time. My home network runs on a MikroTik router I [segmented into six VLANs]({{< ref "posts/2026-08-22-home-network-vlan-design.md" >}}) with a default-deny firewall between them, and DNS for the whole house goes through [a pair of AdGuard Home instances I run in Docker]({{< ref "posts/2026-08-22-adguard-home-docker.md" >}}). None of it is necessary. All of it taught me something I've since used at work.

This site is the same idea applied to publishing: a [hand-rolled Hugo project]({{< ref "posts/2026-08-21-hugo-without-a-theme.md" >}}) with no theme, no JS framework, and no bundler, deployed to GitHub Pages on every push. If you're curious how it's put together, that post walks through the whole layout.

## What I work on

- Backend services and systems design
- Infrastructure and DevOps — Docker, CI/CD, and the glue between them
- Home networking and self-hosting — VLANs, DNS, and the occasional 2am firewall mystery
- Developer tooling
